## About the Lab

**Pedroxious Lab** is the digital laboratory and experimental environment created by **Pedro Azevedo (Pedroxious)**, Creative Technologist, Web Developer, and 3D Generalist.

More than a brand, the Lab is a research and development space dedicated to building immersive, interactive digital experiences. At its core is the fusion of design, mathematics, and code, bringing animations, real-time graphics, and interactive web development to life.

## Core Pillars

**Visual Research & CGI**
Complex 3D modeling and hard-surface development, from sci-fi vehicles to character design, paired with advanced computer graphics exploration and brand visual identity work.

**Cinematic Production**
A signature for cinematic content: videos and cinematics inspired by pop culture universes, blending visual storytelling with technical precision.

**Interactive Web Development**
Front-end engineering focused on real-time graphics, motion, and immersive browser-based experiences.

**Cloud Rendering Infrastructure**
GPU-accelerated cloud infrastructure for rendering high-complexity 3D scenes and animations at scale, overcoming the limits of local hardware.
